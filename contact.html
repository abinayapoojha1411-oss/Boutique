Travel Guide

How to Reach Us

Alibaug offers scenic connectivity by water and road from both Mumbai and Pune.

From Mumbai (By Sea & Road)

M2M Ro-Ro Ferry: Ferry from Bhaucha Dhakka to Mandwa Jetty (60 mins, cars permitted). From Mandwa, it is a scenic 15–20 min coastal drive to our gates.

Speedboat: 20-minute private speedboat from Gateway of India to Mandwa Jetty.

From Pune (By Road)

Take the Mumbai-Pune Expressway via Khopoli exit towards Pen and Poynad to Alibaug (approx. 140 km, 3.5 hours drive). Scenic views through the Sahyadri foothills.

Airport Connectivity

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Airport (BOM): ~105 km via road or 60 min to Gateway + ferry.

Navi Mumbai International Airport: ~75 km via NH-66. Private chauffeur transfers arranged on request.
