Sanctuary of Serenity

Where Time Slows Down and Nature Embraces You

Tucked away near the meandering riverbend of Alibaug, The Hidden Courtyard is a lovingly restored coastal homestay designed for those who appreciate understated elegance, handcrafted spaces, and genuine Konkani hospitality.

Here, morning begins with fresh birdsong and hot artisanal filter coffee on your shaded verandah. Our central laterite stone courtyard and lush tropical gardens create a secluded haven just 20 minutes from Mandwa Jetty.
